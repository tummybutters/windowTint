# Website page content

pages.json contains the 150 planned public page records. Change copy here, then run the site build. URLs are stable identifiers. Sections use plain-text heading and body strings. FAQ entries use question and answer. Internal links use root-relative paths.

The 53 existing sitemap addresses are retained. Existing facts and copy were read from the November 8 production baseline. New building-film service pages describe offerings already present on the residential and commercial hubs. Existing quote logic remains separate from the content records.

The proposed 20 city case studies had no verified job records. They are published as clearly titled project-planning guides at their planned URLs, with no claims of a completed installation at that location. Replace a planning guide with a case study only after a real project, photo provenance and publishable facts have been verified.

City pages describe preparing a service request and confirming the installation location. They make no claims about a local office, a city-specific testimonial, a completed local job or guaranteed availability. City coverage and search demand should be reviewed against actual inquiry and job data.

The shared offer component owns the full Driveway terms. Product performance, glass compatibility, legal requirements and warranties require the applicable product or official source. Image assignment is left to the shared renderer unless a record has a verified subject-specific image. TintWiz gallery images must not be attributed to one of these cities without corresponding project evidence.
