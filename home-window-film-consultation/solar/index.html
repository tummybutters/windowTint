Before the visit

Will film make my room darker?

Different films let through different amounts of visible light. Compare samples on the actual window and look through them from where you sit.

Can film go on double-pane or Low-E glass?

Compatibility depends on the glass, coatings and film. We check the pane and review the manufacturer’s guidance before recommending a film.

How is the installation price calculated?

Window size, film selection, access and any old film removal affect the estimate. We measure the glass and price the rooms you choose.
